About the Society

Matha Sree Mandiram Charitable Society is a registered non-profit organisation dedicated to the establishment, maintenance and management of Matha Sree Mandiram and other religious places for worship. The Society was formed with the highest ideals of service, devotion and community welfare.

Situated at Gajuladinne Village, Gonegandla Mandal, Kurnool District, the Society strives to uphold Hindu traditions through daily poojas, homams, bhajans, pravachanams and spiritual discourses, while also serving the underprivileged through Annadanam, education and social welfare programmes.

🕉️ Aims & Objectives

Our Core Mission

The Society is established to serve the community through spiritual, cultural, educational and humanitarian activities for the welfare of all.

🛕

Religious & Spiritual

To establish, maintain and manage Matha Sree Mandiram and other religious places for worship. To conduct daily poojas, homams, bhajans, pravachanams and spiritual discourses for the benefit of devotees and the public.

🎭

Cultural Promotion

To promote and preserve Indian culture, traditions and heritage. To celebrate Hindu festivals like Navaratri, Sankranti, Sri Rama Navami, Shivaratri and to conduct cultural programmes for community enrichment.

🍱

Annadanam & Seva

To provide free Annadanam, prasadam, drinking water and other essential services to devotees, poor and needy people without any discrimination of caste, creed or religion.

📚

Education

To run Vedic schools, yoga classes, sloka classes and moral value education for children and youth, nurturing the next generation in Indian values and spiritual knowledge.

❤️

Charitable Activities

To help poor, orphans, widows and senior citizens by providing medical aid, education support and financial assistance, ensuring no one in the community is left behind.

🌿

Social Welfare

To conduct blood donation camps, health camps, tree plantation and cleanliness drives for the betterment of society and the environment we share together.

🏗️

Temple Maintenance

To collect donations and maintain temple infrastructure, renovation and development works ensuring our sacred places of worship are preserved for future generations.

🎉

Festival Celebrations

To celebrate Navaratri, Sankranti, Sri Rama Navami, Shivaratri and other Hindu festivals, bringing together the community in joy, devotion and spiritual togetherness.

👩‍💼

Women & Youth Empowerment

To conduct skill development programmes and self-help groups for women and youth, empowering them with knowledge and resources for a self-reliant life.
